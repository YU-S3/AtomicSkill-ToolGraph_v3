### 1. Context Snippet Mining (Jeopardy! Dumps & Flashcards)
- Many context snippets come directly from raw trivia databases, such as GitHub Jeopardy repositories (e.g., `jedoublen/jeopardy/..._Qs.txt`), J! Archive, or flashcards (e.g., StudyStack, Quizlet).
- **The Pipe and Comma Delimiter Rules**:
  - Look for patterns like `CATEGORY | Clue text | Answer` or `CLUE TEXT, ANSWER.` or `[PAR] ... CLUE, ANSWER.`.
  - The text immediately following the final pipe (`|`) or flashcard comma separator is almost always the canonical exact-match answer.
  - *Example*: `... of "The Maids" | Genet.` -> extract `Genet` (strip trailing period).
  - *Example*: `ROCK "E" | In titles it preceded Eddie Money's "Nights"... | Endless.` -> extract `Endless`.
  - *Example*: `TO COOK FOOD IN LIQUID JUST BELOW THE BOILING POINT, IT'S A WAY TO PAMPER YOUR EGGS, CODDLING.` -> extract `coddling`.
- If an exact clue dump exists in the context, extract that precise answer token directly.
