# GLM short-rule search v11

This manual, exploratory Bank searches independent alternatives for field boundaries,
quantity qualifiers, causal scope and percentage submission. It retains the previously
screened phone clause. Only the core and one publicly triggered clause are delivered.
No task IDs, gold answers or outcome-dependent dispatch are used by the Bank.

`percent_render` alone enables an explicit `percentage` submission kind, and only on
questions routed to that clause. Its Program joins whitespace before a single percent
sign after a digit, preserving every other character, qualifier and unit. It does not
convert rates, check visual reading, select fields or request another model turn.
Previous Bank revisions and their formatting Programs remain unchanged.

The authorized search reuses existing same-interface results as historical controls.
Only novel candidates receive new paid calls. Effects are scored on the actual clause
scope; unrelated-task fluctuations cannot qualify a clause. Selected combinations are
checked before two complete Test201 repetitions. The agreed operational target is at
least 187/201 in each repetition, with a difference of at most two correct answers.
That target is not a statistical proof of deployment stability or a fresh held-out test.

Cumulative authorization: 25M tokens / 4500 HTTP. This batch is separately capped at
6M tokens / 1200 HTTP, with every previous charge and unresolved reservation carried.
Results and immutable plans live in the external experiment delivery directory; no
candidate is represented as a naturally learned Ours asset or a validated gain yet.
